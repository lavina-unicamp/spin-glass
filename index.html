About the 3D Simulation

Note: This simulation is for illustrative purposes only.

  • Particles: Magnetic nanoparticles (MNPs) with dipolar interactions.
  • Arrows: Magnetic dipole moments oriented in 3D.
  • 180° Flips: Stochastic Néel relaxation influenced by relaxation time \(\tau\).

Variables

  • \(q\): Entropic index (interaction strength).
  • \(\tau\): Relaxation time (average wait between flips).
  • \(T\): Environmental temperature.
  • \(T_{cut-off}\): Critical freezing threshold.
